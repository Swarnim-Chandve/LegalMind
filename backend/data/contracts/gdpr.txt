GDPR COMPLIANCE POLICY

Data Processing

Personal data must be processed lawfully,
fairly, and transparently.

Right to Access

Users may request access to all personal
information held by the organization.

Right to Erasure

Users may request permanent deletion of
their personal data.

Data Breach Notification

Any significant data breach must be reported
within the legally required timeframe.

Compliance Officer

A Data Protection Officer shall oversee
privacy compliance activities.
