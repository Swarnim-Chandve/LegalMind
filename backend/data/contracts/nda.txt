NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Confidential Information

The receiving party shall keep all confidential
information private and shall not disclose such
information to any third party without prior
written consent.

Permitted Disclosure

Confidential information may be disclosed only
when required by law or authorized in writing.

Term

This agreement shall remain valid for 2 years
from the date of execution.

Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by
providing written notice.

Liability

Any unauthorized disclosure may lead to legal
action and financial penalties.
